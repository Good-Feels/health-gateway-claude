# A Good Sign for Claude

**See how your sleep, workouts, and activity have changed.** Ask Claude how
this week compares with last week, whether your resting heart rate is above
your usual range, or how consistent your workouts have been. A Good Sign
connects the Apple Health data you choose to sync from Apple Watch and other
apps or devices that write to Apple Health (HealthKit).

## What you can ask in Claude

- **Sleep and sleep stages:** Compare total sleep and time in REM, Core, or
  Deep sleep across nights when those stages are recorded.
- **Workouts and fitness:** Review running, cycling, strength training, and
  other recorded workouts. Compare workout frequency, duration, step count,
  or active calories across weeks.
- **Heart rate and recovery trends:** Compare heart rate, resting heart rate,
  and heart rate variability (HRV) with your own recent baseline. Ask about
  recorded blood oxygen or weight trends when those data are available.

Try asking: “Compare my sleep duration and HRV over the last two weeks. What
changed, and which days are missing data?”

## What you can see on iPhone

Open the A Good Sign app for your **estimated Sleep Score**, workout heart
rate zones, and personal bests when enough data is available. Those app views
are separate from the synced metrics Claude can query.

## Connect A Good Sign

1. [Get A Good Sign on iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6805944274), activate
   your subscription, and sync the Apple Health categories you want to use.
2. Add the plugin in Claude on the web, then connect it using the same Apple
   Account you use in A Good Sign. Follow the [setup guide](https://agoodsign.app/setup#claude).

Claude can read the data you authorize. You can review or revoke the connection
from the iPhone app. A Good Sign describes recorded wellness trends and does
not diagnose conditions or provide medical advice.
